## Users

People with celiac disease in India and caregivers, documenting suspected gluten-related packaged-food labelling issues and sharing concerns with their community. The product owner moderates the invited demo.

## Product Purpose

Give the community a voice through documented concerns, external complaint preparation, community visibility, and traceable responses. Collective visibility may encourage brand accountability; this is an outcome hypothesis, not a promise.

## Positioning

FoodProof complements official complaint portals with an evidence-backed community record. It does not replace official filing or guarantee brand/government action.

## Operating Context

User-reported interview findings: some people do not know official portals exist; others receive no response and fall back to brand emails or WhatsApp communities. FoodProof joins label evidence, action preparation, and follow-up around individual product concerns.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Public introduction page; invited demo feed and reporting; user/reviewer test identities; private drafts; label photos covering identity/claim/ingredients plus explanation for publication; optional batch/receipt; explicit sharing consent and moderation; manual external submission/response records; closure/reopen independent from safety or resolution.

Phase one uses sample/redacted data and mocked identities. Real email OTP, phone OTP, Google sign-in, and RBAC move to phase two before public launch. English now; reminders/translations later. Separate government and brand histories. No auto-sending, safety scores, comments, likes, or fabricated impact.

## Product Principles

- Evidence is distinct from allegation and response.
- Community publication is distinct from external submission.
- Private content becomes shared only through explicit review and consent.
- Demonstrated usability is distinct from real-world accountability outcomes.
- Keep the first release narrow and suitable for a same-day demo build.
